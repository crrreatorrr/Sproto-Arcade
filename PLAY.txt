SPROTO ARCADE • THE LONG WAY HOME

Open index.html in a desktop browser. The game runs offline.
Choose a Sproto portrait and a starting level.

GROUND FIGHTS
Left / Right (or A / D)   Backpedal / advance
Space (or Up / W)         Jump. Press again in midair to double jump.
Down (or S)               Crouch
Shift                     Dash
C                         Tracking missile, 2-second recharge
V                         Tracking bomb, 3-second recharge
X                         Shield for 1 second, 10-second recharge

Your Sproto faces right and fires horizontally on its own.
Hold Down to dodge Ceiling Sweep. Your weapons still work during the sweep.
Ground columns are Phase 2 hazards. In the last two quarters of Phase 2,
watch for SHIELD NOW and press X to block the arena pulse.

LEVEL 3 • MUDLINE TRENCHES
Use all four arrow keys or WASD to fly. The saucer fires green shots
automatically. C extends the red laser arm, V fires a bouncing beach ball
from the rear launcher, and X shields the saucer for 3 seconds.

LEVEL 11 • ROOFTOP BREAKAWAY
Your Sproto runs automatically. Use Left / Right to adjust your pace.
Jump, double jump or dash across the gaps and dodge Goldie's jetpack shots.
Falling or getting caught ends the run. Reach the exit to clear the campaign.

Escape                   Pause or resume
9, 9                     Toggle God Mode
Z                        Toggle auto-fire and weapons in God Mode
I                        Toggle God Mode damage between 6× and 20×

Health refills at the start of each level. A retry restarts the current level.
