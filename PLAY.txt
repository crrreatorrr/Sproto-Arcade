SPROTO ARCADE — MACHINE TRIALS PROTOTYPE

Open index.html directly in your desktop browser. No server or network needed.
Choose encounter, pilot, seed and full run or phase/sector practice, then Start.

MOVE: arrows / WASD (four directions while flying)
JUMP: Space; press again for air jump
DASH: Shift; remap using the button below the game
SHIELD: X — shield-symbol attacks only accept shield
STANDARD SPECIALS: C piercing beam / V bouncing ball (recharging base kit)
PICKUP POWER: F; E while touching a pickup deliberately replaces your slot
RETRY: R (restores standard kit and current full/practice entry)
PAUSE: Escape; focus loss also pauses
MENUS: arrows / W S move, A D or left/right change a selector, Enter or Space
confirms, Escape closes the pause screen

CONTROLLER: plug in and press a button. Stick / D-pad move, A jump, B or RT dash,
X or LT shield, Y C special, RB V special, LB pickup power, View replace pickup,
Start pause. Menus: D-pad moves, A selects, B resumes. See README.md "Controls".

Broken chevrons mark attacks requiring an active dash. Ready indicators and
remaining power charges appear in the HUD. Read the current objective: an
armored target may require a valve, relay, reflection or successful counter.

Full clears offer the next encounter. Practice results are kept separately
by phase and sector. Same seed and inputs reproduce simulation behavior.

Original campaign: legacy.html. Reference review: art-review.html.
Placeholder machines are intentional. Production animation and human balance
validation remain gated; see docs/GATES.md and README.md for evidence.
