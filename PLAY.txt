SPROTO ARCADE • THE LONG WAY HOME

Extract the whole folder and open index.html in a desktop browser.
The game runs offline. Choose a level and advance right.
Four of the ten stages end in a boss fight.

Left / Right (or A / D)   Backpedal / Advance
Space (or Up / W)         Jump. Hold for more height.
Down (or S)               Crouch
Shift                     Dash through danger, on the ground or in the air
Escape                    Pause

Golden Sproto always faces right, even while retreating. The boss stays
on the right. Getting into its space deals contact damage.

Weapons
- Gold: automatic horizontal fire. Jump to hit exposed weak spots.
- Cyan: press C for a tracking missile. Recharges in 2 seconds.
- Green: press V for a tracking bomb. Recharges in 3 seconds.
  The bomb arcs toward an enemy and explodes on the floor.

Boss projectiles and hazards are placeholder boxes. Pink floor markers
warn where columns will rise. Clear the marked lanes before they erupt.
Defeat each giant to advance. Health refills at each new level and boss gate.
A rematch starts at the current boss. Bot Sales is the final fight.

The original Sproto fighter animations play at giant scale. The campaign
uses ten panoramic backdrops that reflect at their edges for a continuous loop.
