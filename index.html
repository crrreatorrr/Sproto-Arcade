Machine and weapon reference review ↗

Broken chevrons mark dash-only fronts; shield symbols call for X. Your current objective and failure source appear during play. Phase practice resets to the standard kit. Weapon charges carry between phases in a full run. Every attempt uses a fixed simulation step and the chosen seed.

Boss art follows each encounter’s pilot and machine references. Phase 2 adds slow, distinct projectiles. Human comprehension and final balance testing remain open.
