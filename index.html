Machine and weapon reference review ↗

All machine silhouettes and effects in this build are placeholders. Broken chevrons mark dash-only fronts; shield symbols call for X. Your current objective and failure source appear during play. Phase practice resets to the standard kit. Weapon charges carry between phases in a full run. Every attempt uses a fixed simulation step and the chosen seed.

Production animations require timing, critical pose, and hit-area approval. Human comprehension and final balance tests remain separate release gates.
