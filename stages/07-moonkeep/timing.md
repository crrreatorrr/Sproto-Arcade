# Moonkeep / Packet F — Jet flight

Level 7 is a jet encounter in both phases with four-direction flight controls. Big Swole's two dragons flap their wings while their cockpits and moving targets follow the patrol. In Machine Trials, Phase 1 has a 72 px patrol amplitude over about 299 ticks. Phase 2 has an 85 px amplitude over about 262 ticks. The player's bounds are X80–820 and Y240–620.

In Phase 1, Iron Fang locks the player's position at cycle tick60, winds up60, bites18, and recovers30. The dragon fires a low breath at tick120 and a high breath at tick177. Each shot locks the pilot’s horizontal position 27 ticks before release and launches from the moving mouth. The neck joint is open at ticks138–234 of each240-tick cycle, with12HP. Changing altitude evades the locked bite. The first actual joint hit emits the introductory reward.

The high breath releases at tick28 and the low breath at tick82, each with an altitude cue. Iron Fang returns at tick660 and repeats every300ticks. The slow missile requires a completed counter plus60ticks of settling. Breath finishes with a low strike, and the body cannon aims high before firing low at the same locked horizontal position. Retreat from the cannon when flying close to the dragon. The player uses the jet’s free-flight controller in both phases. The missile streak follows the Story shot lane. The bouncing heavy charge flashes at contact and expands into a violet shockwave.
